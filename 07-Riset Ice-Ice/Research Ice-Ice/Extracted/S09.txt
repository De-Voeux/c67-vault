                                                 by both abiotic and biotic factors manifested by the presence of white and soft parts in the
                                                 infected seaweeds. The occurrence of this disease varies from species, places, and seasons.

                                                 pre-soaking with antibacterial substances and nutrient enrichment before out-planting,
                                                 and possibly using genetic engineering.
