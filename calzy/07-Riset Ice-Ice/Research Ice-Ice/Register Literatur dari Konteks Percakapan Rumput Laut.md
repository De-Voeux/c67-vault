### Evidence hierarchy for future agent work

1. **Direct quantitative findings from a specific paper** (with design, species, location, sample size, dates, units, statistics).
2. **Authors' interpretations** and study limitations.
3. **Across-paper synthesis**, explicitly discussing contradictions and external validity.
4. **Field interviews/local observations** with timestamps/location when possible.
5. **Unverified hypotheses** proposed during brainstorming.

Never blur these levels. Quote or cite a study only after reading the corresponding PDF.
